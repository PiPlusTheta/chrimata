# Brag Plan for Chrimata

## Core Concept
**Angle:** Trust is good, but verifiable evidence is better. Stop relying on outdated decks.
**Hook:** A bold claim ("₹2.4cr ARR!") gets instantly fact-checked and corrected by raw ledger data in real time.
**Highlights:**
1. Ingests raw source evidence (PDFs, ledgers) and extracts facts.
2. Auditable financial calculations that update live.
3. Automatically flags discrepancies between founder claims and actual data.
**Punchline:** "Chrimata: Due diligence with hindsight."
**Tone:** `polished` (Serious, elegant, restrained)
**Visual Identity:** Dark theme (institutional, expensive, editorial as per AGENTS.md), clean typography, highlighting the contradiction in numbers.

## Storyboard (~20s)
1. **Scene 1 (0-3s): The Hook.** 
   - Visual: A sleek dark card appears with text "Claim: ₹2.4cr ARR".
   - Audio: A deep synth swell.
2. **Scene 2 (3-7s): The Reveal.** 
   - Visual: A new document (April ledger) slides in. The "₹2.4cr" turns red and strikes through. Text updates: "Actual: ₹12L active MRR. Flagged: Unsigned pipeline included."
   - Audio: A crisp, satisfying "thud" or digital confirmation sound.
3. **Scene 3 (7-12s): The Engine.**
   - Visual: Fast-paced typing/code reveal of the audit trail: "Hindsight agent cross-referencing..." showing a clean JSON/graph of the issue history.
   - Audio: Fast, rhythmic ticking building up.
4. **Scene 4 (12-16s): The Solution.**
   - Visual: A polished dashboard view showing the full truth: 4-month runway, flagged claims, resolved issues.
   - Audio: Warm, resolving chord.
5. **Scene 5 (16-20s): Punchline & Outro.**
   - Visual: Fade to logo/name "Chrimata. Due diligence with hindsight."
   - Audio: Final deep bass drop.
